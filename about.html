About us

Breathing better air, all year round.

A Salem workshop built on skill, honest service and 25 years of happy customers.

Kovai Car A/C was founded in 1999 and has been running successfully for the past 25 years, based in Salem. Our hands-on experience in this field counts up to 30 years.

We have been honoured with the “Best Customer Service” award by the Salem Mayor, selected from across Salem, Namakkal, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri and Villupuram.

Helping customers breathe better air throughout the year, we provide a professional auto air-conditioning service and repair facility with market-leading products and fully qualified staff — designed to be cost-effective.

We offer a variety of A/C services to the general public and the garage trade, all intended to keep your A/C system running smoothly. With a fully fitted workshop and fully qualified staff, our clients enjoy a cool A/C system at all times, with comprehensive service that meets your growing demands.
